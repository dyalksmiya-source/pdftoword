How to cancel your subscription

You can cancel at any time:

  • from this page, with the "Manage subscription" button (it opens Paddle's secure subscription page), or
  • by emailing support@pdf2wordly.com.

Cancelling stops the next renewal. Pro stays active until the end of the period you already paid for, then your account returns to the Free plan. Your account and its data are kept until you delete your account. You can get a full refund within 14 days of any payment: see the Refund Policy.

Delete account

Deleting your account permanently removes your sign-in, your email address, your plan and your usage information. It cannot be undone. See what is deleted and what is kept.

If your Pro subscription still renews, cancel it first with Manage subscription.

Delete your account?

This permanently deletes:

  • your sign-in and email address,
  • your plan and usage information,
  • the link to your Paddle subscription.

If your Pro subscription is set to end later, the remaining Pro time is lost. Payment records stay with Paddle, our payment provider.
