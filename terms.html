The service

PDF2Wordly converts PDF files into editable Word documents (.docx), including scanned PDFs through text recognition (OCR) in English, French and Arabic. You upload a PDF, we convert it on our servers and you download the result.

Your account

You need a free account to convert files. Provide a valid email address, keep your password private and tell us if you think someone else is using your account. You are responsible for activity on your account. One account is for one person.

Free plan

  • 1 conversion every 24 hours
  • Up to 20 pages per file
  • Up to 25 MB per file

The Free plan costs nothing and does not require a payment method.

Pro plan

Pro costs $4.99 per month (USD), plus any tax that applies in your country. Pro includes:

  • Unlimited conversions
  • Up to 200 pages per file
  • Up to 25 MB per file (the same file size limit as the Free plan)
  • Text recognition (OCR) for scanned PDFs

Billing and automatic renewal

Pro is a monthly subscription. It renews automatically every month and you are charged at the start of each monthly period until you cancel. We will tell you in advance, by email, about any price change; the new price applies from your next renewal, and you can cancel before it does.

Cancellation

You can cancel at any time, with no cancellation fee:

  • from your Account settings page ("Manage subscription", available when Pro launches), or
  • by emailing us at support@pdf2wordly.com.

Cancelling stops the next renewal. Pro stays active until the end of the period you already paid for; after that, your account returns to the Free plan. Your account and its data are kept until you delete your account (see Suspension and deletion below). To get money back, see Refunds.
