How to Convert PDF to Word

A simple, three-step walkthrough for turning any PDF into an editable Word document, right in your browser.

Why convert a PDF to Word

PDF files are great for sharing a document exactly as it looks, but they're not built for editing. Converting a PDF to Word (.docx) gives you a file you can actually edit: fix a typo, update a figure, or reuse a paragraph in a new document, without retyping everything from scratch.

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Upload your PDF. Open the converter and either drag your file into the drop area or click "Choose PDF file" to select it from your device. Files up to 25 MB are supported on the free plan.
  2. Start the conversion. Click "Convert to Word" and the tool will rebuild the text, layout, and formatting of your PDF as an editable document.
  3. Download your Word document. Once the conversion finishes, click "Download Word" to save the .docx file to your device. You can open it in Microsoft Word, Google Docs, or any compatible app.

No installation needed. The whole process runs in your browser and works the same way on desktop, tablet, or mobile.

Converting scanned PDFs

Scanned documents are essentially images of text rather than real, selectable text. The converter can still process these files, but the accuracy of the result depends on the scan quality. For best results, use a scan that's straight, well-lit, and high resolution.

Tips for a cleaner result

  • Use the original PDF whenever possible, rather than a scanned copy, for the most accurate formatting.
  • Unlock password-protected PDFs before uploading, since encrypted files can't be read directly.
  • Review tables and multi-column layouts after conversion, as complex layouts sometimes need small manual adjustments.
  • Keep a copy of the original PDF until you've confirmed the Word version looks the way you expect.

Frequently asked questions

Will the formatting stay the same?

The converter aims to match the original layout as closely as possible, including fonts, spacing, and images. Very complex layouts may need minor touch-ups after conversion.

Is it free?

Yes. Converting a PDF to Word is free, with a daily conversion limit. The Pro plan removes that limit and supports larger files.

Are my files kept private?

Files are sent over an encrypted connection and aren't made publicly accessible. Temporary files are deleted automatically once the conversion is complete.
