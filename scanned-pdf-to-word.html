Know your file

Is your PDF actually scanned?

Digital PDF

Made from Word, Google Docs, or a website. You can select and copy the text directly. No OCR needed — the standard converter handles it.

Scanned PDF

Made from a scanner, printer app, or phone camera. Every page is really a photo — trying to select text does nothing. This is what OCR is for.

Not sure which one you have? Just upload it — the converter checks automatically and switches to OCR only when it's needed.

The process

What happens after you upload

    Upload your scan

  2. 02

  3. 03

Get a cleaner result

Tips for better OCR accuracy

  • Scan at 300 DPI or higher — blurry or low-resolution photos produce more reading errors.
  • Keep the page straight; heavily tilted scans confuse word order.
  • Even, flat lighting works better than a phone photo with shadows or glare.
  • Handwriting isn't reliably supported — OCR here is built for printed text.
