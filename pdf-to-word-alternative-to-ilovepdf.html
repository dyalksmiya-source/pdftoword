What people usually look for in a PDF to Word tool

When the task is just "turn this PDF into an editable Word document," most people care about a handful of things: is it free, does it require an account, does it leave a watermark, how accurate is the conversion, and does it handle scanned documents as well as digital ones.

How PDF2Wordly compares

iLovePDF is a full suite: merging, splitting, editing, e-signatures, workflows, and more, alongside PDF to Word. PDF2Wordly does one thing — PDF ⇄ Word conversion — and keeps the process short.

Frequently asked questions

Is PDF2Wordly the same as iLovePDF?

No. iLovePDF is a broad suite covering dozens of PDF tools, from merging and editing to e-signatures. PDF2Wordly focuses specifically on converting PDF files to Word, including scanned documents with OCR.

Do I need to create an account to convert PDF to Word?

No. You can upload a PDF and download the converted Word file without signing up or logging in.

Does PDF2Wordly support scanned PDFs like iLovePDF's OCR tool?

Yes. Scanned or image-only PDFs are converted using built-in OCR, with support for English, French, and Arabic text.

Is PDF2Wordly free to use?

Yes, converting PDF to Word is free, with a daily conversion limit. A Pro plan is available for unlimited conversions and larger files.
