Convert PDF to Word on Mac, iPhone, and Android

PDF2Wordly runs entirely in your browser, so you can convert a PDF to Word on a Mac, iPhone, Android phone, or any device without installing software — upload your file, convert it, and download the .docx.

Why device support matters

Not everyone has Microsoft Word or Adobe Acrobat installed, and not everyone is at a desktop computer when they need to turn a PDF into an editable document. Because PDF2Wordly is a browser-based tool rather than downloadable software, it works the same way on a MacBook, an iPhone, or an Android tablet — there's no separate "Mac version" or "mobile app" to look for.

Converting a PDF to Word on Mac

  1. Open the converter in Safari, Chrome, or any browser on your Mac.
  2. Drag your PDF into the drop area, or click "Choose PDF file" and select it from Finder.
  3. Click "Convert to Word," then "Download Word" once it finishes.
  4. Open the .docx file in Microsoft Word, Pages, or Google Docs — all of which run on macOS.

No installation, browser extension, or plugin is required at any step.

Converting a PDF to Word on iPhone and Android

The same converter works in mobile browsers. On a phone, the "Choose PDF file" button opens your device's native file picker instead of a desktop file browser:

  1. Open the converter in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android).
  2. Tap "Choose PDF file." On iPhone this opens the Files app, where you can pick a PDF saved locally, in iCloud Drive, or shared from another app. On Android it opens your device's file picker or Downloads.
  3. Tap "Convert to Word," then "Download Word" to save the .docx to your device.

After you download the file

The output is a standard .docx file. On mobile, it typically saves to the Files app (iPhone) or your Downloads folder (Android), where you can open it directly in the Microsoft Word app, Google Docs, or another compatible editor already installed on your phone.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to install anything to convert PDF to Word on a Mac?

No. PDF2Wordly runs in your browser, so on a Mac you can open Safari or Chrome, upload your PDF, and download the converted Word file without installing any software.

Can I convert a PDF to Word on an iPhone?

Yes. Open the converter in Safari, tap "Choose PDF file," select a file from the Files app or a recent download, then convert and download the .docx to your device.

Does the converter work on Android?

Yes, in any modern mobile browser such as Chrome on Android. The steps are the same as on iPhone: choose the PDF, convert it, and download the Word file.

Will the downloaded file open in the Word app on my phone?

The output is a standard .docx file, so it opens in the Microsoft Word app, Google Docs, or any other app on your phone that supports Word documents.
